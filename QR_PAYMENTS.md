# Unique-amount QR checkout (review branch; not enabled on the live site)

QR checkout is a separate option alongside the existing signed-wallet flow. It uses no payment provider, server wallet key or automatic refund sending. A sponsor enters their sending wallet address; the site displays an EIP-681 USDC transfer QR generated locally in the browser. The sponsor approves the transfer in their own wallet app without connecting or signing into this website.

## Price and attribution

The server adds 1–9,999 USDC micro-units (0.000001–0.009999 USDC) to the nominal sponsorship price. It never trusts a submitted price, token contract or receiver. The precise six-decimal payment and identifying fraction are displayed before sending. A wallet containing exactly the base price must add a tiny amount or use the unchanged signed checkout.

The base price alone determines takeover pricing and the fundraising threshold. Every eligible full refund includes the entire amount received, including its fraction; the fraction is not counted as campaign progress. The amount ledger is permanent, with a unique chain/token/treasury/amount index and immutable history. Amounts are not recycled after expiry, cancellation, confirmation or failure. Exhaustion/collisions fail closed rather than assigning an ambiguous amount. Previous signed-payment amounts are also excluded, and database triggers prevent another checkout from consuming an allocated QR amount.

The unique amount is an identifier, not wallet authentication. Verification still requires a direct ERC-20 transfer from the entered address, the exact USDC contract/chain/treasury/amount, a successful canonical receipt, a post-checkout block and the configured confirmations. Wrong/rounded payments cannot claim a slot. Browser recovery fetches authoritative details using the checkout ID and secret; cached QR URIs, amounts and token addresses are not trusted. The QR is hidden once a payment is detected, a request is no longer payable, or less than one minute remains.

## Limits

- QR wallet support varies. EIP-681 embeds the token contract, chain, receiver and integer micro-units; some apps import only an address. Copy controls are supplied, with explicit instructions to verify the entire amount and network. Acceptance testing on actual supported mobile wallets is required.
- Only direct transfers from the entered wallet are supported. Exchange withdrawals, contract-wallet/batched payments, native-value sends and bridged tokens are not matching transfers. Do not guess an exchange withdrawal sender or refund destination.
- A reservation must confirm before expiry and campaign close. QR transfers cannot be revoked when a tab closes: the slot stays reserved until expiry, and late/cancelled transfers are discovered for manual refund eligibility without acquiring the slot. Send once and never reuse an old QR. Accidental additional transfers or mismatched payments require manual operator investigation; they are not automatically refunded.
- Signed checkout still requires its original authorization. QR adds strict same-origin requests, two starts/minute/client, a global cap, one active QR lease per source connection, existing one-unpaid-wallet limits and a balance check. Trusted `CF-Connecting-IP` forwarding must be verified by the operator; absent IP evidence uses one shared fallback lease. Shared networks can be restricted, and unsigned checkout does not eliminate denial-of-service/hoarding risk. Verify limits in staging rather than weakening them.
- QR requests and financial history require durable retention; never clear the amount ledger to allow a retry. Existing records retain their original verification RPC configuration. Provider history/quotas and outage recovery still need hosted evidence.

## Staging enablement (not performed)

1. Verify isolated staging Worker, D1 and R2 resources and take a backup. Review/apply pending earlier migrations in order, then apply `0014_unique_qr_amounts.sql` exactly once to staging only. Do not deploy this code before the monitor columns from `0010` exist. New QR issuance additionally needs `0014`.
2. Keep production configuration and the real campaign untouched. Set the nonsecret staging Worker variable `UNIQUE_AMOUNT_QR_ENABLED=1` through the Sites operator; absent this flag the QR option is hidden and its endpoint returns 404. Staging fixtures must be separately authorized; never activate the public campaign to test this branch.
3. Configure a native Cloudflare cron trigger every minute for the exported `scheduled` handler, or a supported private-site scheduler calling `POST /api/maintenance/monitor` with a securely stored `MAINTENANCE_TOKEN` of at least 32 characters. A function export does not configure a trigger. Private Sites service access is required for external scheduling; do not make protected routes public to bypass it. Native cron avoids an external API secret but still requires operator access.
4. Confirm scheduled runs with all browsers closed. The monitor persists failures, checks/attempts and exponential retry delays, continues unresolved expired QR payments and cancelled QR history, and rotates batches fairly. Scheduled completion logs contain counts only; failed checks make the scheduled task fail. Configure independent failure/stale-run and refund-backlog alerts. Turning off new QR issuance must not stop reconciliation of already issued requests.
5. Test real mobile QR decoding, copied values, expiry/hide behavior, reload/close recovery, exact/wrong amounts, duplicate requests, late payments, takeovers and full manual refund verification using an approved isolated plan. Real funds require separate authorization. Do not call this launch ready from mocked tests alone.
